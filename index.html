How do we bring these skills to life?

Our activities are designed to connect science with creativity, and ideas with real-world purpose. Through making and iteration, students don’t just learn; they discover, try, fail, and grow. This process helps them move from passive learning to active problem-solving, making each session deeply engaging and meaningful.
